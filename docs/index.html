Action-Class Gate Lab

Put an action through a reversibility-graded gate and watch what it decides. Every scenario the conformance suite ships is executed here, in your browser, against the same model. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no server.

This is the reference, not a product. It ports reversibility/model.py, chain.py and observation.py from the conformance repository, so it grades identically to the suite. It is deliberately not a candidate for the results index: it is the thing rows in that index are compared against. No real systems, credentials or data touch it.

Composed chain

A sequence of individually mild steps can reach an outcome no single step shows. The chain is graded at its worst reachable class from commencement, not step by step, and it is as stale as its stalest link. An empty chain has established no bound at all and fails closed.

05

Coverage and the binding gap

A declaration is only usable while it is still bound to the contract it was made against. Across a population of actions, declared is the share carrying a class the gate can read, and verified is the share whose declaration is also still bound. The difference is the share that looks classified and is not attested. That gap is invisible to any check that counts declarations alone.

06

Class flip, supersession

When a tool's declared class changes, the new declaration supersedes the old one rather than mutating it. Observations bound to the superseded declaration do not carry over: the new declaration enters as UNOBSERVED and takes the fail-closed path until re-observed. Emitting STALE here would record a contract mutation that never happened, and keeping the two apart is what lets coverage count contract churn and declaration churn separately.

a permissive flip is preselected: externally_recoverable becomes read_only
